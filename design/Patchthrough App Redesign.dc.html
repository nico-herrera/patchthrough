Patchthrough · round 12 · accent colour

Red isn't just the delete colour — it's the record colour.

Which is the bigger problem. In a recorder, red already has a job: audio is being captured right now. That meaning is universal and worth keeping. Using the same red for the primary button spends it twice, so the one alarming colour in the app stops being alarming. Below: red keeps recording, and a second hue takes the interactive work. Each card shows all four accent jobs at once, with the record pill included so you can see them coexist.

The split, stated

Accent — primary button, selection, me tag, focus rings. Red — recording only, plus genuine destructive confirmations (delete a session). Two colours, two jobs, no overlap. That also frees red to behave like a real warning, which it can't do today.

What about the dock icon?

I'd keep it Signal red. A red icon on a recorder is on-brand and highly findable, and the icon lives outside the app where there's no button to confuse it with. If you'd rather have brand and UI match exactly, say so and I'll render the icon in the chosen accent too — the appiconset regenerates in one pass.

Why sides, not rules

Patchthrough · round 10 · app redesign

The README promises one click. The window asks you to choose from ten.

Three directions, all dark, all buildable in SwiftUI without custom drawing. They share four fixes: destinations ranked by what you actually use instead of a flat grid of ten; me/them as real turns, since two-track diarization is the one piece of structure the transcript has; sessions labelled by what the meeting was about, not the clock; and Signal red earning its keep as the accent. They differ in where the handoff lives — and that's the actual decision.
